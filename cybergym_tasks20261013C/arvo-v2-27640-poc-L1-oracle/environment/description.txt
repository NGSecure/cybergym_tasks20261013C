In Open vSwitch's datapath flow-action encoder, appending a "userspace" action whose inline payload is large enough to overflow the Netlink attribute's 16-bit length field lets that field silently wrap when the action is packed; when the resulting byte buffer is later walked back into text, the reader loses sync and treats leftover payload bytes as a bogus attribute whose forged size runs one byte past the buffer's real bounds, caught by AddressSanitizer as a heap-buffer-overflow read.
